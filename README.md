### 🧭 About Me

I am a **Technology Executive, Enterprise Architect, and Hands-On Builder** with **30+ years of experience** across financial services, insurance, retail, and enterprise software. 

While my background includes serving as a **CTO** and **Group Manager** leading multi-disciplinary engineering and BI teams, I have never stepped away from the keyboard. Today, I focus on architecting resilient, high-throughput backend systems, **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** integrations, and **AI-driven enterprise platforms**—combining decades of architectural discipline with modern ecosystems like **Java 25**, **Jakarta EE 11**, and **Spring AI**.

- 🔭 **Currently building:** AI-powered enterprise analytics & guardrailed agentic workflows (**Gaia**), multi-tenant SaaS administration consoles, and high-concurrency enterprise archive migration tools (**IntraVault**).
- 🧠 **Core Philosophy:** Clean separation of concerns, database-enforced security, and pragmatic architecture over hype.
- 🎓 **Background:** B.S. in Mathematics Engineering (İTÜ) & B.A. in Business Administration — fluent in English, French, and Turkish.
